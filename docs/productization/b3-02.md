## 1. What is enforced

```text
decision policy   a challenge opens ONLY on a frozen-WatcherV2 fraud verdict WITH recorded
                  detection evidence (root-phase facts, Freivalds residual, cheap-op
                  mismatch — quoted verbatim into the decision); a bare fraud verdict is
                  refused; probability is never part of the wording
bond management   the challenger balance is checked against the bond floor BEFORE any
                  transaction; a shortfall is an explicit refusal naming both amounts
window tracking   the chain's challenge window is a height; anything inside the safety
                  margin is refused (WindowClosed semantics in the decision record)
dispute driving   the frozen state machine is polled (claiming -> bisection -> arb_ready ->
                  resolved); the driver only records what the chain says and never
                  invents moves
outcome handling  ChallengerWins / WorkerWins / BothInvalid / timeout are recorded from
                  the chain's dispute state
on-chain evidence every challenge and every resolved dispute carries a sha256 transcript
                  digest, and the full transcript is written to the journal dir
duplicate guard   the dispute ledger refuses a second challenge for a task with an
                  active dispute (B3-03's ledger, enforced here too)
```
